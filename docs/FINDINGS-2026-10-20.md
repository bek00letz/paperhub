# Findings, 20 October 2026: why Paperhub stalled

Paperhub's central idea was a touch dashboard on an e-reader that stays current
for weeks on a battery. Before building more of it, we tested that idea on a real
device. It didn't hold up.

## What was tested

An **Onyx Boox Go 6** (Android 11, Boox firmware 4.2), with a small test app
([experiments/sleep-screen-test](../experiments/sleep-screen-test)) that shows a
dashboard, wakes on a timer, and records the battery's charge counter at every
event. Measured overnight with the front light off.

## What we measured

| State | Drain | A full charge (~1,500 mAh) lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Awake and touchable, idle | about 20 mAh per hour | about 3 days |
| Asleep, with a background check every 15 minutes | under 2.5 mAh per hour | weeks |

The charge counter moves in steps of about 15 mAh, so these figures are
approximate. The direction isn't in doubt: asleep is roughly eight times cheaper.

## What the device allows

- **Asleep, the screen can show the dashboard.** Onyx's "Transparent" sleep
  screen keeps whatever was on screen when the device fell asleep, at no cost.
- **Nothing an app does can change the screen while it sleeps.** Onyx's power
  manager refuses every app-requested wake-up, including the system routes apps
  normally use (the alarm-clock "full-screen" route). An app can still draw, but
  the drawing only reaches the e-ink when someone presses the power button.
- **Touch can't wake it.** Only the power button can.
- **The sleep screen can't be replaced.** The setting naming it can be written,
  but Onyx writes its own value back at every start.
- **"Stay awake" lasts about 95 minutes.** After that the Boox sleeps anyway.
- **Background work does run while asleep:** timers fire on time, and an app can
  download and store a new frame. On Boox this needs auto-freeze off and the app's
  background restriction lifted (from a computer); Boox leaves that restriction in
  place even after freezing is switched off.

## What that leaves

- **In your hand:** asleep, it shows the last thing you saw, for weeks. Press
  power and it's current at once, because the app kept the data fresh in the
  background. That works today.
- **On a wall:** plugged in, always live and touchable. Battery doesn't matter.
- **On battery, current while asleep:** not possible on this device without
  rooting it.

That last case was the whole point of Paperhub, and it's exactly what hardware
built for it already does. TRMNL-style screens sleep for months and wake on their
own timer. [e-paper panel](https://github.com/bek00letz/epaper-panel) supports
them, so that's where the work went.

## What a new approach would have to solve

- A vendor whose Android lets an app wake the screen, or draw to the e-ink while
  asleep. The Viwoods AiPaper (Android 13) is untested.
- An optional add-on for rooted devices that makes Paperhub the sleep screen.
- Anything that makes "always current on battery, and touchable" work without
  asking people to flash or root their device.

The [code audit from 19 October](AUDIT-2026-10-19.md) lists the known problems in
the code as it stands, for anyone who picks it up again.
